[SECTION 1: PAID TIME OFF AND ATTENDANCE POLICIES]
All full-time employees accrue Paid Time Off (PTO) at a rate of 1.5 days per completed calendar month, totaling 18 PTO days per financial year. Additionally, 12 days of paid Sick and Casual Leave (SCL) are granted on January 1st annually. Casual leave requests exceeding 3 consecutive business days require Departmental Director sign-off at least 5 business days in advance. Unutilized PTO up to a maximum of 8 days may be rolled over into the subsequent financial year; any accrued PTO beyond 8 days at the end of the fiscal year is automatically encashed at the base daily salary rate on March 31st payroll. Failure to report for 3 consecutive days without prior notification constitutes voluntary abandonment of employment.

[SECTION 2: PARENTAL, ADOPTION, AND BEREAVEMENT LEAVES]
Primary caregivers, regardless of gender, are entitled to 26 consecutive weeks of fully compensated Parental Leave following the birth or legal adoption of a child under the age of 16. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 consecutive weeks of fully paid leave, which must be utilized within 6 months of the qualifying event. In tragic circumstances of miscarriage or pregnancy termination, 6 weeks of fully paid medical recovery leave is mandated. Compassionate bereavement leave of up to 5 consecutive working days is granted for immediate family members (spouse, children, parents, siblings), and 3 days for extended family members (grandparents, in-laws).

[SECTION 3: REMOTE WORK, HYBRID SCHEDULES, AND WFH REIMBURSEMENTS]
Employees in designated hybrid roles are required to be physically present in their assigned regional office for a minimum of 2 designated anchor days per week (typically Tuesdays and Thursdays). Fully remote contracts require Chief People Officer (CPO) approval. A one-time Home Office Ergonomic Stipend of up to $500 is reimbursable upon submission of valid commercial receipts for desks, ergonomic chairs, external monitors, or laptop docks within the first 90 days of employment. A recurring monthly Internet and Utilities Allowance of $50 is credited directly via payroll to eligible remote and hybrid personnel.

[SECTION 4: HEALTH INSURANCE TIERS AND MEDICAL BENEFITS]
The corporate group health insurance scheme provides comprehensive medical coverage underwritten by Global Health Assurance Corp. Level 1 (Associate to Senior Specialist) provides $50,000 baseline family floater coverage (Employee, Spouse, up to 2 Dependent Children). Level 2 (Manager to Director) provides $100,000 floater coverage including dependent parents. Pre-existing conditions, critical illness coverage, and maternity hospitalizations (up to $7,500 normal delivery, $10,000 C-section) are covered from Day 1 of policy inception with zero waiting period. Outpatient Department (OPD) dental and optical expenses are reimbursable up to $500 per fiscal year upon submitting GST/tax invoices.

[SECTION 5: MENTAL HEALTH, WELLNESS, AND EMPLOYEE ASSISTANCE (EAP)]
All staff and their immediate family members have 24/7 confidential access to the Employee Assistance Program (EAP) providing up to 8 free tele-counseling sessions per year with licensed clinical psychologists. The company observes one paid 'Wellness Recharge Day' per quarter. Employees may expense up to $300 annually for gym memberships, yoga studios, meditation app subscriptions, or sports club facilities under the Corporate Lifestyle Wellness Benefit.

[SECTION 6: PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLANS (PIP) AND GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL]
Employees receiving an annual performance rating below 'Meets Expectations' or demonstrating documented competency deficiencies may be placed on a structured 60-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). The PIP document must specify clear, measurable milestones reviewed bi-weekly by the direct manager and HR Business Partner (HRBP). Successful completion results in formal closure; failure to meet documented benchmarks at the end of 60 days results in immediate contract termination with 30 days severance pay or pay in lieu of notice.

[SECTION 7: CODE OF CONDUCT, ANTI-HARASSMENT, AND ETHICS REPORTING]
The organization maintains a zero-tolerance policy regarding workplace harassment, discrimination, bullying, and sexual harassment (POSH compliance). All reported incidents are formally investigated within 14 business days by an Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) consisting of internal HR leadership and an independent external legal counsel. Retaliation against any whistle-blower or complainant is strictly prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate summary dismissal. Anonymous reports can be filed via the dedicated Ethics Hotline (ethics-hotline@company.internal).
